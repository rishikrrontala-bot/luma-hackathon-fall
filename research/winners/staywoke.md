## Pitch, verbatim (as indexed)
> An "AI-powered mobile application designed to tackle drowsy driving" that "combats drowsy driving by using facial recognition and voice interaction to detect driver fatigue in real time and issue alerts."

## The wow moment
On-camera, real-time fatigue detection: the phone watches the driver's face and reacts. A live, visual AI moment.

## Scope reality
- Shown (per results page): face-based fatigue detection, voice interaction, alerts.

## Stack
On-device computer vision on a phone (face recognition). The stack **is** part of the story: AI running live.

## Submission page shape
Technovation submissions include a pitch video, technical video, business plan and screenshots. The rubric scores ideation, technical, pitch and entrepreneurship: **the closest rubric to LUMA's four 25% criteria that we could find.**

## Why this won (one sentence)
A visceral, everyday danger, solved by a live on-camera AI moment a judge understands in two seconds, backed by a separate technical walkthrough and a business plan.

## Transferable to us
- A **live, visual, on-device vision moment** is exactly the demo shape to copy: point the camera, the AI reacts, numbers appear.
- Keep the **technical walkthrough as its own clean segment** (LUMA mandates it).
- Beginner-division winners won on clarity, not complexity. Our technical depth has to stay legible.
