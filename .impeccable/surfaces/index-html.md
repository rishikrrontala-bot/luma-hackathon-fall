Audience: teens and young adults buying and selling secondhand clothes on their phones; judges on laptops with no garment to hand. Job: know whether a listing will fit before paying; sellers need measurements for a listing.

Grounded list, ordered by resonance (seed bdbff07a, degraded roll: no challengers reachable): 1 tech-pack points-of-measure sheet · 2 Depop/Vinted flat-lay listing grammar · 3 sewing-pattern tissue and envelope (the name's literal reading) · **4 thrift-store tag system** (assigned) · 5 cutting mat with dual cm/in rulers · 6 woven size and care labels · 7 tailor's tape.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The thrift store's own tagging system turns measurement into merchandise: every dimension the tool finds is shot onto the garment as a tag on a barb, and your closet is a rack sorted by size dividers. It refuses the category default (white SaaS, phone mockup, "AI-powered" badge) and its dark neon-scanner opposite.

OWN-WORLD: "This week's colour": one fluorescent tag stock (chartreuse by default, rotating by ISO week through pink, orange, green and sky) drenches the Persuade bands, always with black ink on it. Die-cut tags with punched holes, plastic tagging-gun barbs as connectors, price-gun stickers for micro-labels (± values, units), round rack size dividers as chips and tabs, a register receipt for pricing. A heavy condensed signage face, a hyperlegible workhorse body face, tabular stamped numerals. Operate surfaces sit on cool, fluorescent-lit white with the tag colour as the one accent.

STORY: See a garment get measured in one second → understand "a sheet of paper is the ruler" → believe it because the ± and the draggable points are visible → measure your own or try a sample → save to your closet → check a listing against it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left ~58%: a flat-lay photo (synthetic sample, labelled) with dimension lines drawing themselves and four tags swinging in on barbs (PIT TO PIT 56.2 cm ± 0.4). Right: the headline in the signage face at display scale ("Know it fits before you buy it"), one sentence, primary action "Measure a garment" plus secondary "Try a sample". The chartreuse field owns the whole band. Phone: the photo stacks first, then the headline and actions stay above the fold.

FORM: Thrift-store tag system, position 4 of the ordered list, seed key bdbff07a. Signature interaction: tagging (line draws → barb pierces → tag swings and settles, spring physics, reduced-motion = instant).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
